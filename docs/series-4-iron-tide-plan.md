# Series 4 - Iron Tide Protocol

## Goals

- Push the style toward tough, robotic, and battle-ready creatures.
- Increase legendary density compared with previous series.
- Add a fish/fishing identity to the series tab icon.
- Ship a roster between 70 and 150 creatures.
- Add fresh catch-level music so Series 4 sounds distinct.
- Add new buddy unlocks tied to Series 4 completion and progression milestones.

## Final Scope

- Total new creatures: **70**
  - 5 regions x 14 creatures each
  - Per region: 8 common, 4 rare, 2 legendary
- Total Series 4 legendaries: **10**
